# ArcEvents firearm bursts and per-item tuning, 2026-11-18

The stock trigger retains its existing profile. The optional queued trigger starts up to the number of rounds selected on the held item, bounded by its magazine capacity and available loaded rounds. Each actual round consumes one paid magazine round in Survival or Adventure. A canceled future round consumes zero. The existing 1:1 iron nugget recipe, reload time and capacity apply. Creative preserves its loaded and reserve quantities, including on return to Survival. Match ammunition requires the match ID and world guns accept only ordinary ammunition. Cadence assumes 20 TPS, repeated stock inputs or a running selected burst, sufficient loaded rounds and free scheduler capacity. Reloads and interruptions reduce actual sustained throughput. A full Five-Seven burst can expend 20 rounds in one trigger input. Its 19 shot gaps take 114 ticks. Stopping a burst or changing slots has to preserve the interval after the last round fired. One Double Barrel round still creates a ten-pellet volley and spends one magazine round. [Bundled profiles](../../src/main/resources/config.yml)

Under the fixed low/base/high scenarios, 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 rounds per active player-day consume the same number of iron-nugget equivalents both ways. With 1 / 5 / 20 participants the totals are 700 / 35,000 / 1,400,000 equivalents per network-week. The [calculator input](firearm-burst-20261118.json) uses 1 / 2 / 4 active hours and 100 / 500 / 2,500 shots per hour. Direct mint and burn are each zero for Vault, and each zero for tokens, per player-day and network-week. The optional tracked nugget purchase at 2.22 Vault and alternatives from existing inventory, mining or farms are unchanged. There is no new acquisition price or exchange to estimate. SELL and the planned transition to contracts are unchanged. These assumptions hold use fixed while the easier trigger can change real ammo use.

The admin permission can change damage per pellet within 0.1–40 and spread within 0–30° on the specific held item. An ordinary tuned item can retain its metadata when transferred. This creates no new grant, price or broadly applied multiplier. It can materially change damage per paid round. At the upper configured damage, a Five-Seven round can deliver 40 HP compared with its stock 5 HP before native armor, immunity and protection. Native world mob kills can still reach existing job or quest hooks. Ordinary-world kill rate, reward eligibility on the active servers, frequency of tuned items and incremental job or loot income are unmeasured. Owned expedition actors use the existing removal path before native death reward hooks. Expedition equipment persists inside its own inventory profile and exit restores the origin inventory and XP. [Expedition baseline](apocalypse-workbenches-20261115.md)

TTT keeps +1 local credit for a qualifying enemy kill, capped at 64. Correct attribution can change who receives that credit or the recorded kill. Gun Game advances on a kill attributed to the current stage weapon; reaching the knife stage and winning grants match results and persistent stats. Zombies keeps its 150-point resupply, 650-point mystery weapon and 1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000-point upgrade path with +35% of base damage per level. All three power levels cost 6,000 Zombies points for +105% base damage. Fishing offers 32 rounds for 8 expedition credits. Ammo, catches, gear, credits and upgrades in these modes are scoped to that match. Owned enemies and catches bypass the native reward path and the normal player-state restoration returns the origin inventory. Geometry, participation and easier combat can affect local progress without changing the configured offers or award schedules. [Zombies baseline](firearms-zombies-20261104.md)
