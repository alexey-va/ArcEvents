# Ordinary-world firearm ammunition, 2026-11-01

Historical baseline: [the 2026-11-02 Creative exception](creative-firearms-20261102.md)
supersedes this document's all-mode ammunition requirement for Creative only.

Before this change the 12 mapped vendor guns only offered cosmetic aiming outside events. Afterward their existing firearm specifications drive actual shooting. The new world magazine starts empty; one ordinary iron nugget loads one round. Guns are usable individually, retain their vendor metadata and persisted rounds, and cannot load from event ammunition. Reload consumption and the resulting magazine are committed in one owning-thread inventory write after revalidation. Cancelled reloads consume nothing. No free magazine, cash award, shop, recipe, exchange or automatic refund is added.

## Separate units and scenarios

The JSON contains explicit low/base/high assumptions: 1/5/20 players, 1/2/4 active hours daily and 100/500/2500 rounds per active hour. Daily item consumption is 100/1,000/10,000 iron nuggets per player; network weekly consumption is 700/35,000/1,400,000 nuggets. Direct incremental vault and premium-token mint/burn is zero. Items are not converted to money in this calculation. XP and entitlement payouts remain unchanged.

Tracked Spawn/Survival EconomyShopGUI configuration quotes a buy price of 2.22 vault per iron nugget and sell -1 (`plugins/EconomyShopGUI-Premium/shops.yml`, IRON_NUGGET entry). These values were not reverified live or changed here. If purchased at that existing unit price, the scenarios imply 222/2,220/22,200 vault of optional daily expenditure; existing stocks or iron farms can supply ammunition without those purchases. Full magazines therefore optionally cost 2.22–106.56 vault for 1–48 rounds. This is not a new enforced price or a claim about the shop's treasury routing. Gun acquisition prices and normal repeatable acquisition paths are unknown; no progression saving-time claim is made.

## Indirect effect and decision

Existing configured damage per pellet, spread, range, magazine sizes, cooldowns and reload durations remain unchanged. Ordinary-world damage was previously zero because these vendor items did not fire. Shotgun pellets are accumulated per volley/target and each head/body collision is weighted separately, whereas rapid bursts retain native hurt immunity. This also corrects the old event shotgun path: individual same-tick damage calls could lose most pellets to native hurt immunity. A full body volley can now total 27 HP for DOUBLE_BARREL (10 × 2.7) or 18.8 HP for VEPR_12 (8 × 2.35), before armour/native protection, rather than approximately one cold-hit pellet of 2.7/2.35 HP. This is a material combat-throughput correction; actual kill/reward throughput remains unknown. Fast bullets do not load chunks or break blocks. Damage retains the real shooter and passes through ordinary native protection/reward listeners; PvP-disabled worlds and event participants/arenas are fenced separately. Existing team and region cancellation is never cleared.

Ordinary-world ranged kills can improve manual mob-kill efficiency. Its incremental reward rate has not been measured, so no estimate of extra vault/XP is presented as fact. The new path adds neither kill bonuses nor a money multiplier and remains constrained by existing reward eligibility and rates. The source emission delta is zero; throughput effects remain a runtime observation gap. Do not invent a new cap, price or access restriction as a substitute for measurement. Existing EconomyShopGUI SELL is preserved; its planned transition to contracts remains a future scenario.

The accepted change is an item sink with no free ammunition. Focused checks cover reload cancellation, item movement, stacked or malformed guns, event-ammo isolation, cooldown and native damage handoff. Post-activation acceptance must still observe actual protection cancellation and representative native first-person gunplay. No monitoring or later restart is scheduled by this assessment.
