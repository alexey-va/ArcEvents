# Pirate Cove · Disasters

GoldenOre created **Pirate Cove**. AlmightyBread contributed feedback and building help. This adaptation uses version 1.0.2 from Overcast Community. [Original map](https://github.com/OvercastCommunity/PublicMaps/tree/1e8cf8b79218ee13f82a172ee6193ffca6834040/ffa/pirate_cove) · [Author and contributor credits](https://github.com/OvercastCommunity/PublicMaps/blob/1e8cf8b79218ee13f82a172ee6193ffca6834040/ffa/pirate_cove/map.xml)

RusCrafting converted the terrain, ship, reef and jungle blocks to Java 1.21.11. The glass sea became water. The adaptation adds the storm wharf, copper-roofed boat shed, a stepped gangway onto the ship, a sandstone lookout and a lighthouse on the offshore rock. The original PvP setup, entity data and container contents were removed.

`scene.schem` is an editable Sponge v2 schematic, 128 × 76 × 128 blocks, with its minimum corner at `(-71, 50, -61)` in `arcevents_disasters_coast`. Original block `(x, y, z)` was placed at `(x − 35, y + 46, z + 23)` before the gameplay edits.
