ArcEvents uses the store and parking lot from `region/r.0.0.mca` (SHA-256 `edba07e57f49130db5698710e4445ea7fe8bc22d214b47309da402ed3c89250d`). The crop spans `(144,90,256)` through `(251,111,380)` inclusive. Paper 1.21.11 converted the original block states. The adaptation removes the original actors, inventories, barrier walls and game logic, substitutes inanimate shelf stock, lights the cross aisles, recesses one shelf end at the team entrance, closes the loading doors, and adds six barricaded entrances and four working supply stations. `scene.schem` is the editable Sponge v2 map.
